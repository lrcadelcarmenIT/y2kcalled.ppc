# Soundboard audio sources

The museum soundboards play audio recordings and original app/system clips. Embedded ID3 metadata has been removed from every MP3 before publication. Source credits are retained below.

The static WAV and modem OGG were converted to MP3 for browser playback. Other audio streams were copied without resynthesizing the sounds.
